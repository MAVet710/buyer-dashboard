## Issues found and addressed

- Operation links initially scrolled to a solution button without selecting the corresponding content. Hash synchronization now selects the intended operation.
- Cultivation wording initially suggested broader harvest records than the reviewed workspace establishes. It now names growth phases, estimated harvest dates and plant history.
- No empty customer-proof section or dead resources were added to simulate commercial maturity.

## Conversion assessment and honest gaps

Primary beta conversion appears in navigation, hero, product, solution, controls and final section. Secondary inspection goes directly to the workspace, with full-size image access. Beta participation and data-use links explain the next step. Existing login remains a separate action.

The dark/orange identity, short headline and generous spacing are visually distinct from benchmark sites. In the reviewed desktop image, the product frame starts near the bottom of the first viewport, so visitors scroll to inspect most interface detail. The explicit workspace action provides that route; do not claim this has empirically improved engagement.

The page gives operators enough context to assess a beta conversation. It cannot erase the absence of approved customer outcomes, a professional walkthrough, additional operation-specific captures or public assurance/legal content. These are real remaining assets. A mature-company appearance must not be achieved by pretending beta is general availability. Customer interviews and privacy-conscious funnel data are the next evidence needed to assess conversion performance; no lift is claimed here.
