## Corrections made during review

1. Replaced broad cultivation harvest-record language with plant records, room context, growth phases, estimated harvest dates and plant history. The existing harvested phase does not establish a complete harvest-management product.
2. Corrected solution hash navigation so an operation link selects its matching content instead of leaving the default vertical content displayed. This prevents the page from answering the wrong buyer question.

## Proof and resource gaps remain explicit

Approved customer proof data is empty; therefore there are no rendered testimonials, customer logos, case studies or quantified results. The footer does not invent security, privacy, terms, state-guide or case-study destinations. Participation/data-use content is labeled as such and is not presented as a published privacy policy. Standalone approved legal/security pages and public educational resources remain content work, not hidden completed deliverables. No biography or operator-employment claim was added. No unsupported claim should be upgraded because a route exists or a test passed.
